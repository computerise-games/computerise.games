Voxel Worlds v0.2.0

All worlds, ships and stations now have 3D voxel models!

Voxel worlds

  • Every world with a surface map becomes a ball of voxel cubes up close, with the same spin, day and night, city lights and clouds. Switch it in Settings > Display > Voxel Planets.
  • Saturnus's rings are drawn live in 3D under the tilted camera.
  • The 40 ships nearest the camera are voxel models, wherever they fly.

Launches and landings

  • Booster launches lift off on voxel booster stages sized to the ship, the plume scaled to match.
  • Parachute canopies are voxel domes, and the landing flame scales with the hull.
  • Booster prices scale with the ship they lift: Drones get their own cheaper boosters, Freighters and Starliners pay double, Carriers and Colonies triple.

Camera

  • Cinematic mode hides the trajectory's apsis, collision and preview markers.
  • The closest-approach marker is now orange.
  • Controller: tap R3 to snap back to the top-down view on your ship.

Interface

  • The mass bar rings a category red when a load won't fit.
  • Controller hints name the station button, Warp and Pause in flight; panels open on their first control; the SHIPCOM ticker opens the Log.
  • Long-press tooltips work on buttons inside panels.
  • The version number shows at boot.
  • Opening a dropdown or dialog no longer mutes the game on phones.
