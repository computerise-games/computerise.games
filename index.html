New Worlds

A space-faring universe

New Worlds is a space-faring universe simulation game where you navigate gravity to explore, complete missions, trade, upgrade and improve your ship fleet.

The last great frontier

Adventure to uncharted planets, to settle and develop the “New Worlds” of the last great frontier!

In search of New-Terra

An endless timeline makes the stars and terraforming planets within reach in a search for “New-Terra”.

Living solar systems

Travel to dynamic planets across procedural solar systems, whose atmospheres, resources, markets and populations evolve over time, influenced by player action…or inaction.

Factions

The interests of Factions unfold through politics and diplomacy, as well as aggressive expansionism, in an endless scramble to reap the rewards of the galaxy.

The physics of gravity

The game features a top-down 2D map-mode, allowing players to intuitively navigate space whilst exploring the beautiful physics of gravity.

A 3D world revealed

The camera seamlessly reveals the 3D world around the player, with voxel spaceships, panoramas of moons rising and setting, rings around gas giants, plus eclipses and transits across huge stars.

Trader or emperor

Join a guild, buy a freighter and become a humble space-trader, or create your own faction and race the other powers to build a great space empire!

Free Demo

The demo of the Web version of the game is publicly playable at playnewworlds.com, check it out!

The demo is a locked-down version of the game, with no Multiplayer, a cap on in-game years and some features are gated behind the paid version. Most future features and systems in the game will only be available in the full version, and the game is planned to be in active development and early-access to get user feedback as soon as possible.
