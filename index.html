A space-faring universe simulation where you navigate gravity to explore, trade, complete missions and build up your fleet. Fly to uncharted planets, then settle and develop the new worlds of the last great frontier.

Travel across procedural solar systems whose atmospheres, markets and populations change over time, shaped by what you do and what you leave alone. Factions expand, bargain and fight over the galaxy. Join a guild as a humble space trader, or found your own faction and race the other powers to build an empire.

Fly a top-down map with real orbital physics, while the camera tilts to show the 3D world around you: voxel ships, moons rising and setting, ringed gas giants, eclipses and transits across huge stars.
